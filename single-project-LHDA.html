Las hijas del Arte

  • Branding
  • Identity Design
  • Typography

Full visual identity for Las hijas del Arte, a cultural project that reclaims the role of women in art history.

The project covered logo design, typographic system, color palette and social media applications, creating a coherent identity with its own character.

Every visual decision responds to the project's values: reclamation, elegance and modernity, with references to classical art reinterpreted from a contemporary perspective.
